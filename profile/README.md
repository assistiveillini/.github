# Assistive Illini

**Building technology for accessibility.**

Assistive Illini is a Registered Student Organization (RSO) at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)** dedicated to developing innovative technologies that improve accessibility and empower individuals with disabilities.

We bring together students interested in engineering, computer science, robotics, and design to create practical, impactful solutions to real-world challenges.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make assistive technology more accessible through student-led innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and hands-on engineering.

We aim to provide students with opportunities to apply their technical skills while developing solutions that can make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

## Current Projects

### 1. American Sign Language (ASL) Interpreter

Developing a system that uses computer vision and machine learning to recognize and interpret American Sign Language gestures.

**Focus Areas:**
- Computer vision and gesture recognition
- Machine learning and artificial intelligence
- Real-time interpretation
- Accessible user interfaces

### 2. Prosthetic Arm

Designing and developing a functional prosthetic arm that integrates mechanical engineering, electronics, and software to explore accessible prosthetic technologies.

**Focus Areas:**
- Robotics and mechanical design
- Embedded systems and microcontrollers
- Sensors and motor control
- 3D modeling and prototyping

## Get Involved

Assistive Illini welcomes UIUC students from all majors and experience levels. Whether you're interested in software development, hardware engineering, machine learning, product design, or project management, there are opportunities to contribute.

**Why join Assistive Illini?**

- Gain hands-on experience building real-world engineering projects.
- Collaborate with students across different technical disciplines.
- Develop skills in robotics, AI, embedded systems, and software development.
- Build technology with a focus on accessibility and social impact.
- Contribute to open-source projects and grow your technical portfolio.
